# Employee Handbook

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive twenty five days of paid annual leave each year. Unused leave can be carried over for up to five days into the following year. Leave requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the people portal, and managers respond within three working days.

## Public holidays

The company observes all national public holidays. Staff who are required to work on a public holiday receive a day in lieu that must be taken within sixty days. Offices are closed between Christmas and New Year, and this closure does not reduce annual leave.

## Sick leave

Employees who are unwell should inform their manager before nine in the morning. A doctor's note is required after three consecutive days of sickness. Sick pay is paid in full for the first ten days of absence in any calendar year and is reviewed by the people team after that.

## Remote work

Staff may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are ten to three on every working day, and team meetings are scheduled inside that window. Employees working remotely must be reachable on the company chat tool during core hours.

## Parental leave

New parents are entitled to sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave. The leave can be taken in one block or split into two periods within the first year. Employees should tell their manager about their plans at least eight weeks before the expected start date so that cover can be arranged.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and in December. Each review covers goals achieved, feedback from colleagues and a development plan. Salary changes are decided after the December review and take effect from the first of January.
