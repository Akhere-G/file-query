# Orbit Pro Headphones FAQ

## Battery

The Orbit Pro battery lasts fourteen hours on a single charge with noise cancelling enabled. A fifteen minute quick charge provides three hours of playback.

## Charging

The headphones charge over a USB-C cable. A full charge takes about two hours and the status light turns green when charging is complete.

## Warranty and returns

Every Orbit Pro includes a two year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Unused products can be returned within thirty days of delivery for a full refund.

## Connectivity

The Orbit Pro supports Bluetooth 5.3 and can be paired with two devices at the same time.
