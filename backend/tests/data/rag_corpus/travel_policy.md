# Travel and Expenses Policy

## Flights

Employees should book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Business class may be approved by a director for longer flights. Flights must be booked through the company travel portal.

## Hotels

The hotel limit is 150 euros per night in most cities and 200 euros in London, New York and Tokyo. Bookings should include free cancellation whenever possible.

## Daily allowance

A daily meal allowance of 40 euros applies on every full day of travel. Alcohol is not reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Claiming expenses

Receipts and expense claims must be submitted within fourteen days of returning from a trip. Claims submitted after that deadline need approval from a finance manager.
